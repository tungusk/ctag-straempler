(REST, panel screenshots, the web page driven in Chrome, the bench with audio +

   the quiet save keeps the whole take; leaving Tape still writes the crop.

6. **FX with an LFO on the CV matrix** — does it sound right?

8. **Knob settings that never used to save** — survive a power cycle
