# Ear-debt checklist — consolidated 2026-07-30

Everything owed to ears (or eyes) across bin/*/README.md, memory, and the open
bug list, in one place. Three buckets: a guided listening session the bench can
drive while Arlo only listens; things automation can settle without ears; and
things that need hands on the hardware (a patch cable, a power switch, a
deliberate mistake).

Cleared before this list was written, for the record: multisample seams on a
real instrument (Moog Voyager G2–G6, 07-28, "sounds pretty good"); loop-by-eye
on Live exercised while setting sustain loops; OD level on Synth and Keys; Tape
crop; flanger+room on Keys; reverb mode-change click (provisional, 07-26).

## A. The listening session (bench drives, Arlo listens)

Ordered so machine switches are few. Each item says what plays and what to
listen for. Estimated 20–30 min total.

### On Keys (as loaded)
1. **One-shot declick.** A drum one-shot with looping OFF, allowed to run out.
   Measured clean (end step 1.00x the material's own slew, was 1.81x). Listen:
   does it end silently, and is the 5 ms taper too soft on a hard transient?
2. **Trimmed loop.** Hold a looping 808CLAP, then 808SN. Measured: repeats at
   the trimmed length, no silence gap. Listen: continuous stutter, no gap — and
   did the -60 dBFS trim cut a real tail? (58.9% came off the clap.)
3. **Loop-drag feel** (soft item — he has used it). One detent = one pixel;
   clicking into an edge ARMS looping. Any surprise is the finding.
4. **Auto-tune on real piano/EP.** Expected ~8–9 cents sharp (stretched
   partials, known). Confirm it lands close enough to trim with Fine.
5. **Overdrive Bias sweep.** The curve changed 07-26 (bias no longer scaled by
   drive gain — over half the knob was a dead zone). Sweep bias at a few drive
   settings. Listen: character change across the whole range, no mute.
6. **Wet/dry balances and FX tails** (07-20 blanket item) — while FX are up,
   flip slots off/on and listen for tails cut short or balance jumps.

### On Drums
7. **OD level.** The 6 dB overdrive trim was global; Synth and Keys confirmed,
   Drums never heard. Kick/snare through OD at a few drive settings.
8. **Per-pad pitch by ear.** ±12 semitones on a pad, retuned live while
   ringing. (V/oct accuracy is bench-measurable, item B5.)

### On Tape
9. **`post` vs `pre` route with Drive up.** `post` colours playback and records
   dry; the tape preamp prints always. Listen for the doubling that the route
   fix removed, and that `off` doesn't dump level.
10. **Crop contents on a monitor.** Crop a loop, play the TCR_ file back —
    confirms the effective-window save (K5 moves + matrix included).
11. **Clock on CV8, drive quiet.** Patch a clock to CV8: the un-wiring of
    K8→Drive means no rhythmic distortion with all FX off. Mechanism confirmed
    07-25; the listen is the sign-off.

### Whenever it fires
12. **Reverb NaN flush.** If `rv.nan` ever goes non-zero, the new incremental
    flush costs ~28 ms of dry reverb instead of a pop. If Arlo hears the pop
    again, read the counter IMMEDIATELY (`/status?fx=1`) — that is the whole
    diagnostic.

## B. Automatable (no ears needed)

1. **Reverb mix-step pop test** — the open suspect: `gd`/`gw` recomputed per
   block from `rv->wet` with no slew. Capture a held note with the mix static
   vs stepped at the same POST cadence; compare burst counts.
   → `tools/bench/mix_sweep.py` (written tonight; result in the report).
2. **Autosave across reboot** (remote path). Set a param, let autosave land,
   `POST /reboot`, read back. NOTE: only covers the REST path — the physical
   knob path (machine_state_dirty → debounce) needs a hand on a knob (C3).
3. **Soak, 3 h** — queued since 07-26. Also the way the NaN counter gets real
   uptime behind it: read `rv.nan` after the run.
4. **Empty-slot cursor / load-failure message by eye** — /screenshot can do it,
   but building the state means clearing zones on the live patch. Do it on a
   scratch patch in a spare moment, not on PAT_001.
5. **Drums V/oct pitch accuracy** — bench capture + pitch_detect per semitone,
   like zone_sweep.py. Worth a script if item A8 raises doubt.

## C. Needs hands

5. **Keys/Tape ear items above** that involve playing feel rather than sound.

## Results so far (added 07-31)
- B1/B3: the overnight soak (85 min recorded, hall reverb on, mix static)
  caught **4 unexplained hits** (~1 per 21 min; biggest 10.8x the material's
  slew ceiling) with `rv.nan` = 0 after 3.5 h — the NaN flush is exonerated,
  and the pop happens WITHOUT mix motion. Clips in `tools/bench/soak/`.
  mix_sweep.py still worth running to see if mix motion raises the rate.
- B2: autosave across `POST /reboot` PASSED (remote path).
- NEW known-red: a multisample preset with reverb on drops its last zone on
  load (tank allocates before the zone arena). Workaround: load with rv 0,
  then re-enable. Fix belongs in preset_load ordering.
